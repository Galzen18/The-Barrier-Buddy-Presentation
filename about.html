About Barrier Buddy

A Canadian manufacturer of aluminum privacy fences and gates for residential and commercial projects — and a proud subsidiary of AluFacade.

Get a Free Quote
AluFacade Group

Privacy, built by facade experts.

As one of AluFacade's subsidiaries, Barrier Buddy manufactures its premium aluminum exterior home products in AluFacade's state-of-the-art factory, located 45 minutes south of Montreal.

That means every Barrier Buddy fence and gate is engineered, extruded-to-length, powder-coated and fabricated by the same team that builds commercial curtain wall and facade systems. Owning the whole process lets us offer very quick turnaround times and competitive, factory-direct prices — whether you're a homeowner, a developer or a contractor on a commercial job.

  • In-house powder-coating line

    We customize the texture, colour and finish of extrusions up to 24 feet long — including woodgrain effects and custom colour matching.

  • Engineering & fabrication

    Our engineers evaluate structural integrity and our team fabricates to suit — up to a single 24 × 8 foot Barrier Buddy segment with our 0.5″ panel separators.

  • Quick turnaround

    Manufacturing everything under one roof means shorter lead times and competitive prices.

  • Certified quality

    All Barrier Buddy products are AAMA 2603 / 2604 / 2605 certified and use Italian Fapim hardware.

Our promise

Built to last. 25-year warranty on powder coat.
