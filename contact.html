        FAQ
        
Good questions

        
Can't find your answer? Ask in the project details of your quote request.

What is the standard Barrier Buddy size?
The Barrier Buddy comes standard as a 6 ft high by 6 ft wide aluminum panel privacy fence in matte black. The standard Barrier Buddy Gate is 4 ft wide by 6 ft tall, also in matte black.

        
Can I get custom sizes and colours?
Yes. Dimensions, panel profile (number of courses), colour, texture and finish are all customizable. We offer 24 standard colours, textures and woodgrains, and can match a custom colour from a reference sample.

        
How long can a single section be?
Our powder-coating line finishes extrusions up to 24 feet long. With a structural review by our engineers, we can build a single 24 × 6 ft Barrier Buddy segment.

        
Do you offer a glass gate?
Yes — the Barrier Buddy Gate is available with aluminum panel infill or 6 mm frosted glass infill, with stainless steel hinges and Italian Fapim hardware.

        
What warranty do you offer?
All Barrier Buddy products come with a 25-year warranty and are AAMA 2603 / 2604 / 2605 certified.

        
Do you work with contractors and developers?
Absolutely. We supply homeowners, developers and contractors for residential and commercial jobs, with shop drawings and factory-direct pricing.

        
Where are you located?
Our head office and factory are in Vaudreuil-Dorion, Quebec, 45 minutes from Montreal. Our Ontario showroom is at 7250 Keele St, Concord, ON.
