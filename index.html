Barrier Buddy fun fact

A 24 by 8 foot Barrier Buddy

As a subsidiary of AluFacade, Barrier Buddy has access to AluFacade's state-of-the-art factory — including a powder-coating line that can finish extrusions up to 24 feet long. With our 0.5″ panel separators and a structural review by our engineers, we can build a single 24 × 8 foot Barrier Buddy segment.
