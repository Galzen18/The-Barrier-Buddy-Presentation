Panel profiles

Course versatility

Change the look of your fence by changing the number of horizontal courses. The 21-course fixed bay is our standard; layouts with 2 to 6 courses use custom face heights.

2 coursesTallest profile, bold lines
3 coursesWide boards
4 coursesBalanced
5 coursesClassic board look
6 coursesNarrow boards
21 coursesStandard — fine horizontal lines

Custom front-face height, profile depth and aluminum wall thickness are separate specifications. Available profiles, span, support and thickness are confirmed for each project. Elevations are schematic.
