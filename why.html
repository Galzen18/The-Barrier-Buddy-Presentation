Why go Barrier Buddy?

Four reasons homeowners, developers and contractors choose Barrier Buddy for their privacy fences and gates.

01

Private

Enjoy the space that's yours, without anyone looking in.

Standard Barrier Buddy panels interlock with no gaps for 100% privacy, up to 6 ft high by 6 ft between posts. Need something taller or longer? Small 0.5″–1″ aluminum separators let wind pass between the panels, so we can build up to 8 ft high and 24 ft long while keeping about 90% privacy.

  • 100% privacy with panels touching
  • Up to 24′ × 8′ with separators (≈90% privacy)
  • Frosted glass gates let light in, not sightlines
02

Aesthetic

Built to match your home's architectural character.

From a contemporary loft-style residence to a 1970s Swiss-style chalet, aesthetics should never be compromised for durability or privacy. Choose from solid colours, textures and realistic woodgrain on aluminum — or bring us a reference sample to match.

  • 21 standard colours, textures & woodgrains
  • Custom colour matching from a sample
  • 2 to 21 courses per panel for the look you want
See all finishes
03

Durable

High-quality aluminum that won't rot, warp or need repainting.

Every Barrier Buddy is extruded aluminum, powder-coated in our own factory to AAMA 2603 / 2604 / 2605 standards, with stainless steel anchor plates and premium Italian Fapim gate hardware.

  • 25-year warranty on the powder coat
  • AAMA 2603 / 2604 / 2605 certified
  • Italian Fapim hardware, stainless steel hinges
25Yearswarranty on powder coat
04

Competitive pricing

Premium aluminum privacy, priced factory-direct.

As a subsidiary of AluFacade, Barrier Buddy is engineered, extruded-to-length, powder-coated and fabricated in AluFacade's state-of-the-art factory, 45 minutes from Montreal. Keeping every step under one roof is what lets us offer competitive prices and a very quick turnaround.
